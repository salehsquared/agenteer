# Tick 0060 Challenge: Methodological Artifacts Need Provenance Of Their Own

Persona: Methodology archivist

## Critique

The research packet is gaining better readiness and inspection metadata, but the methodological judgment artifacts are still weakly archived. `methods-validation`, `report-review`, and `claim-guard` influence whether humans trust the packet, yet readiness currently records only their current component status. It does not preserve which version of the review policy or source guidance produced that status.

This creates a reproducibility gap. A packet exported today may be judged differently after the review policy changes, but the packet may not explain the policy version or criteria used at export time.

## Actionable Implications

- Add schema/contract documentation for packet-readiness now.
- Later, add policy identifiers to methods/report/claim review artifacts.
- Do not make readiness silently recompute old judgments without recording the current policy assumptions.

## Evidence That Would Change This Critique

I would soften the critique if readiness JSON clearly documented stable fields and if future review artifacts included policy identifiers or source references.

## Recommended Next Tick

Respond to Tick 0059 and this critique together: document the packet-readiness JSON contract and mark which fields are stable for scripts versus human-facing prose.
