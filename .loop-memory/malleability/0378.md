# Tick 0378 Capability Rave

## Flexible Capabilities Exercised
- Real public-health/ICU paper generation from bounded GCS-backed Parquet reads.
- Code-dictionary phenotype construction for diagnoses and procedures.
- Aggregate-only artifact persistence with row-cache cleanup.
- Event-count-aware model complexity control.
- Propensity matching as a transparent review artifact rather than a causal proof stamp.
- Trust-layer inspection over generated paper packets.
- Cost receipts and cumulative cost limits.
- Reader-facing manuscript generation with machine-readable QA sidecars.
- Typed methodological warnings for overlap and balance.
- Retry-safe idempotent table reads.

## Capabilities To Promote
- Package the MIMIC paper-series runner as a general `research paper-series` or dataset-adapter-driven command.
- Make propensity artifacts a reusable packet contract across stats and paper routes.
- Add lifecycle/rerun/literature generation to batch runners so `needs_methods_review` is more actionable.

## Capabilities Not Yet Trusted
- Phenotype sensitivity over alternative ICD families.
- Literature context attachment to every MIMIC paper.
- Matched-pair variance and unmeasured-confounding sensitivity.
- Publication-grade manuscript generation with tables formatted for journals.
