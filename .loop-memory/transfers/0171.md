# Tick 171 Cross-Domain Transfer - Clearance Contracts for Weight Domains

## Open Question

How should `paper-run` represent subsample-specific NHANES weights so fasting/lab subsample analyses do not look like ordinary MEC-weight analyses?

## Fields Searched

- Air traffic control: ATC clearances authorize a specific operation under specified conditions, but do not remove pilot responsibility. Source: https://skybrary.aero/index.php/articles/atc-clearance
- Programming-language theory: refinement types move validity predicates into an explicit pre-execution contract. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.07763
- Laboratory chain of custody: sample validity depends on documented sample identity, handling, and representational scope. Source: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/21/1.1149

## Chosen Technique

ATC clearance plus refinement-style preconditions: a runner may receive execution clearance only under the weight-domain conditions declared by the AnalysisSpec, and the output must still disclose the scope/responsibility boundary.

## Structural Mapping

- Flight plan -> AnalysisSpec.
- Controlled-airspace clearance -> `paper-run` execution permission.
- Clearance restrictions -> `surveyDesign.weightRationale` and `surveyDesign.eligibilityNote`.
- Pilot responsibility -> paper/lifecycle disclosure of analytic population limits.
- Chain-of-custody scope -> evidence JSON recording the weight domain and rationale.

## Smallest Trial

`paper-run` now refuses subsample weights such as `WTSAF2YR` unless the AnalysisSpec includes both:

- `surveyDesign.weightRationale`
- `surveyDesign.eligibilityNote`

`paper-qa` now requires subsample-weight papers to disclose the subsample analytic population and requires evidence JSON to carry rationale plus eligibility notes.
