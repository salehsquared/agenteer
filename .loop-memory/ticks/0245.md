## Deliverable

The critique argues that exploration needs an explicit burden/promotion gate before it feeds modeling or paper routes:

- tested-pair count and target-pair count;
- multiplicity risk;
- survey-design candidate detection;
- missingness and sparse category burden;
- possible target leakage/proxy detection;
- question-level promotion status and blockers.

## Evidence

The current golden exploration report correctly separates target-centered associations from the background map, but it does not quantify how many associations were scanned or whether high-scoring variables are proxies/leakage risks.
