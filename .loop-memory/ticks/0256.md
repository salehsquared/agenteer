# Tick 0256 - Add Primitive - Exploration Question Quality

## Scope

Label: `methods`, `QA`, `UX`.

Respond to Tick 0255's end-user clinician critique by separating strongest associations from best candidate research questions.

## Hypothesis

If exploration candidates carry a taxonomy and research-interest score, the actual NHANES proof should stop ranking obvious same-domain/proxy glycemic signals as the most useful research questions.

## Intervention

- Added candidate fields: `taxonomy`, `researchInterestScore`, `whyThisQuestion`, and `avoidAsPrimaryQuestion`.
- Added heuristic variable domains for glycemic, anthropometric, lipid, social/demographic, survey/design, and clinical-status variables.
- Added classification for likely duplicate/proxy, expected same-domain biomarker, plausible risk factor, social/demographic determinant, surprising cross-domain signal, design artifact, and general association.
- Candidate questions now sort by avoid-primary status and research-interest score rather than association strength alone.
- `explore-promote` carries taxonomy and research-interest fields into `modelingPlanSeed`.
- Exploration reports now show taxonomy, interest score, plain-language rationale, and avoid-primary reason.
- Updated docs and regression expectations.
