## Web Grounding

- Query: `cirrhosis ICU mortality prediction MELD SOFA observational study methods limitations primary source`
  - Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28240612/
  - Finding: ICU cirrhosis prognostic work commonly compares MELD/MELD-Na and organ-failure scores with development/validation splits and AUROC-style performance.
  - Applicability: our cirrhosis packet should not overread internal discrimination as validated prognosis.
- Query: `AASLD cirrhosis acute decompensation ICU outcomes prognostic scores MELD official guidance`
  - Source: https://www.aasld.org/practice-guidelines/acute-chronic-liver-failure-and-management
  - Finding: cirrhosis with acute-on-chronic liver failure and critical illness is organ-failure-context dependent.
  - Applicability: first-day labs and MELD-like variables should be framed as severity/prognostic markers, not causal factors.
- Query: `transparent reporting prediction model cirrhosis MELD calibration validation study reporting`
  - Source: https://www.tripod-statement.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/TRIPODAI-Supplement.pdf
  - Finding: prediction reports should explain performance measures, model output, thresholds, validation/evaluation differences, and calibration where applicable.
  - Applicability: cirrhosis mortality reports need validation/calibration evidence before prediction claims.
