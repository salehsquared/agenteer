# Tick 0392: regression and GLM validation with reporting pressure

Axis: web-search
Divergence: web-search
Contribution: methods + QA

## Hypothesis

Regression/GLM routes should run across continuous, binary, ordinal, multinomial, count, skewed-positive, zero-inflated, quantile, and penalized model families while preserving method caveats and figure artifacts where appropriate.

## Web Grounding

- STROBE: observational reports need clear statistical methods, participant numbers, analyzed denominators, and interpretation boundaries. Source: https://www.strobe-statement.org/
- ASA p-value statement: p-values are not effect sizes or practical importance and require transparent reporting context. Source: https://www.amstat.org/asa/files/pdfs/P-ValueStatement.pdf
- TRIPOD+AI: prediction-model reporting should include performance measures and plots, calibration/validation context, and intended-use limits. Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38626948/

## Scope

Validate the regression/GLM family against the local corpus.

## Evidence

- 12/12 regression/GLM routes returned `status=succeeded`.
- All 12 used complete-case N=240.
- Penalized routes emitted the expected `PENALIZED_INFERENCE_CAVEAT`.
- Natural residual/diagnostic figures were emitted for robust linear, logistic, Poisson, negative binomial, zero-inflated Poisson, Gamma, inverse Gaussian, and quantile routes.
- Ordinal, multinomial, and penalized routes produced tabular artifacts without natural figure output in this implementation.

## Verification

Ran the 12 `stats-run` commands from the built CLI and summarized `stats-run.json`, `stats-qa.json`, and `figures.json`.

## Decision

Promote the regression/GLM subset to validated for local standard-table execution, with caveats for penalized inference and with future work needed for richer ordinal/multinomial visual diagnostics.

## Counter-Design Considered

Rejected counting a single logistic regression as representative of regression support. Count, zero-inflated, skewed-positive, ordinal, multinomial, quantile, and penalized families fail in different ways and need separate validation.
