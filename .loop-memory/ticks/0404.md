Axis: challenge-response
Contribution: QA + methods

## Hypothesis

Survival plots need labeled axes and group legends because curves are frequently interpreted faster than the surrounding methods text.

## Intervention

Ran a Kaplan-Meier survival packet by treatment group and upgraded generated survival figures to carry axis labels and a legend title.

## Evidence

- Study packet: `.loop-memory/figure-study-suite-20260509/studies/06-survival-by-treatment/`.
- Figure: `kaplan-meier.png`.
- Figure QA: pass, 1125x750, nonblank ratio 0.0335, x=`Time`, y=`Survival probability`.

## Verification

Manual image review confirmed labeled axes, visible step curves, and a group legend.

## Counter-Design Considered

Rejected accepting a curve with unlabeled `0` and `1` groups as publication-style output. Numeric groups can be okay in data, but the plot metadata must tell the reader what they are.
