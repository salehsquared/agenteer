## Axis

capability-rave / truth, methods, QA

## Hypothesis

Exploration mode will only matter if it survives actual public-health data. A deterministic NHANES extract should reveal whether the burden gate detects survey fields, missingness, plausible target associations, and handoff safety.

## Scope

Primary repo writes only. Read cached MedBrevia NHANES Parquet as read-only inputs. No cloud spend.

## Intervention

Created `.loop-memory/golden/0252-actual-nhanes-exploration/nhanes-adult-exploration.csv` from cached NHANES demographics, anthropometrics, diabetes, and lipids Parquet files. The extract includes source hashes, adult filter, survey fields, lab/anthropometric variables, and derived binary variables.

Ran:

- `research explore --target LBXGH`
- `research explore-promote` refusal without note
- `research explore-promote` held handoff with methods-review note

Actual-data pressure exposed that `WTMEC2YR`, `WTMECPRP`, `WTSAF2YR`, and `SDMVSTRA` were not all excluded as design variables. Fixed `looksLikeDesignOrMetadataColumn` and reran the packet.

## Deliverable

- Actual NHANES exploration extract and manifest.
- Actual exploration report/JSON/questions.
- Handoff refusal and handoff success artifacts.
- Review note.
- Design-variable detection fix in `packages/cli/src/commands/research.ts`.
- Capability-rave note in `.loop-memory/malleability/0252.md`.

## Evidence

Actual exploration after the fix:

- 31,849 adult rows.
- 22 columns.
- 105 tested pairs.
- 14 target-centered pairs.
- 6 survey/design candidate variables.
- 4 high-missingness variables.
- `promotionClearance=hold_for_methods_review`.

Top target-centered signals included fasting glucose, derived elevated HbA1c, diabetes questionnaire response, age, waist circumference, triglycerides, BMI, HDL cholesterol, and income ratio.
